The 1Life Story

Why 1Life Accounting exists

Many small and medium-sized businesses face an unnecessary dilemma: high-street bookkeeping services that record figures retrospectively without offering forward-looking commercial perspective, or national accountancy firms that charge high fees while treating mid-sized companies as an afterthought.

1Life Accounting was founded as a specialised offering within The 1Life Group to bridge that divide. Based in Gilford, Northern Ireland, we operate with a modern, paperless approach that supports clients both locally and across the wider United Kingdom.

Our philosophy is straightforward: Clarity in your numbers. Confidence in your business. When your financial routines are automated and disciplined, you spend less time handling paper and more time delivering for your clients.

Practical, Not Theoretical

Our advice comes from decades of managing real businesses, balancing real cash cycles, and navigating commercial transitions.

Collaborative Partnership

We treat our clients as long-term partners, maintaining accessible, open lines of communication when decisions need to be made.

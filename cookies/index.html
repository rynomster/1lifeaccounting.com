Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how 1Life Accounting uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website (1lifeaccounting.com). We believe in privacy-conscious web design and keep tracking to the minimum necessary to provide a reliable browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, save preferences, and provide aggregated information to website operators.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for navigating the website and utilizing core features, such as secure forms and responsive navigation. They do not gather information about you that could be used for marketing.
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies: When enabled, anonymous metrics (such as pages viewed, duration of visit, and error states) help us improve site navigation and performance. These analytics do not capture personally identifiable information.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time. Please note that restricting cookies may affect your experience with certain features on this website.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at morne@the1lifegroup.com.

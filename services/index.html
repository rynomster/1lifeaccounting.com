People & Payroll

4. Payroll Support & Workplace Pensions

Administering staff payroll across the UK requires adherence to Real Time Information (RTI) standards, auto-enrolment workplace pensions and statutory deduction rules.

We handle the entire cycle: electronic payslips for your employees, pension scheme reporting and prompt RTI submissions to HMRC on or before each pay date.

  • Electronic employee payslips & P60 year-end summaries
  • RTI Full Payment Submissions (FPS) to HMRC
  • Auto-enrolment pension uploads & compliance tracking
